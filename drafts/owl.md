# The Owl: draft copy

Draft 1, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya edited. These lines are now in the content files.

**Who gets it:** accounts that give far more likes than they make posts. The attentive lurker.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Owl (Of the Ghost Owl, Of the Vampire Owl, Of the Monstrous Owl)
- **Genus:** *Strix*, Linnaeus's original catch-all genus for every owl. It is feminine: *Strix spectralis*, *Strix sanguisuga*, *Strix consuta*.
- **Why not *Bubo*:** it is the other obvious choice, and masculine. But in Roman lore the *strix* was also a night-bird of ill omen that drank blood, which makes *Strix sanguisuga* an unusually good name for a Vampire Owl.

## Verdicts

**General**

- `owl.verdict.1` All is surveyed from its perch. The Owl marketh what is pleasing, but sayeth little.
- `owl.verdict.2` For every word it uttereth, it hath nodded at a dozen of another's.
- `owl.verdict.3` Nothing passeth beneath its branch unregarded.

**Ghost Owl** (seen but not heard, or given to long silences)

- `owl.verdict.ghost` It flieth without sound, and is known only by what it hath looked upon.

**Vampire Owl** (feeds on replies and mentions)

- `owl.verdict.vampire` It shall watch long from the branch before it descendeth upon the conversation.

**Monstrous Owl** (stitched from links, reposts and quotes)

- `owl.verdict.monstrous` Its nest is lined with all it hath seen and carried home.
