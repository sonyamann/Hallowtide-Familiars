# The Black Cat: draft copy

Draft 1, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya approved. These lines are now in the content files.

**Who gets it:** accounts with too little posted to read: under 30 posts of their own. About 1 in 8 calibration accounts, and nearer 1 in 6 of the survey list.

**What makes it different**

- **No variant,** so it needs its own epithet, and it has no variant verdicts.
- **Almost nothing is measured.** The only trait usually known is likes given. So the cat's card leans on its always-available lines, and I wrote five of those instead of three, and five verdicts, so that cats don't all get the same card.
- **It must read as a character, not a consolation prize.** Every line treats the silence as the cat's choice.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Black Cat
- **Binomial:** *Felis taciturna*, "the silent cat". *Felis* is the Latin for cat and the classic genus.

## Verdicts

- `black-cat.verdict.1` It hath been watched a long while, and hath given nothing away.
- `black-cat.verdict.2` The scribes set out ink and parchment, and it sat upon the parchment.
- `black-cat.verdict.3` It keepeth its own counsel, and its counsel keepeth it.
- `black-cat.verdict.4` What it knoweth, it knoweth; and what it thinketh of thee, it will not say.
- `black-cat.verdict.5` It was seen once at a window, and the matter hath been discussed ever since.

## Natures

**With a trigger**

| Id | Shown when | Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `black-cat.nature.approves` | Gives many likes for each post it makes (top quarter) | Though it speaketh little, it is free with its regard, and many a creature hath felt its approval without once hearing its voice. |
| `black-cat.nature.withholds` | Gives few likes for each post it makes (bottom quarter) | It neither speaketh nor applaudeth, but only watcheth, which is the oldest of the arts. |

**Always available**

- `black-cat.nature.general.1` It is not that it hath nothing to say. Thou hast not earned it.
- `black-cat.nature.general.2` Of the hours the Cat spendeth watching, no record is kept, for it leaveth none.
- `black-cat.nature.general.3` It cometh when it pleaseth, not when it is called. No longer do the scribes call.
- `black-cat.nature.general.4` The Owl is said to see everything. The Cat seeth as much, and maketh less of it.
- `black-cat.nature.general.5` Nine lives are attributed to it. The Cat hath shewn us but part of one.

## Notes

- **Seven natures, not eight,** because only one trait can trigger. The two triggered lines are the high and low ends of it.
- **The Owl line** is the cat's entry in the running joke.
