# Midjourney prompts: the 20 familiars

Draft 1, Oct 6, 2026. One prompt per result, in the vintage die-cut style.

## How to use these

1. **Start with one creature and get the style right.** The Vampire Toad or the Black Cat is a good first test.
2. **Use the image you like as a style reference for all the rest,** so the twenty look like one set.
3. **For a creature's three variants, use its first approved image as a character reference** (`--cref` or `--oref`, depending on your Midjourney version), so the Ghost Owl and the Vampire Owl are the same owl.
4. **Save the keepers into the project folder** and I'll separate the colours and put them on the cards.

## What each prompt asks for, and why

- **Flat colour, clean edges, no texture.** Grain was what hurt thumbnail legibility, and flat colour separates cleanly into layers the renderer can re-ink.
- **Two colours on solid black,** matching the variant's palette on the cards: green and cream for ghosts, red and amber for vampires, purple and acid green for the monstrous.
- **The same pose for all three variants of a creature,** so they read as one animal in three costumes.
- **The art guardrails are in the `--no` list:** no widow's peak or medallion on vampires; no neck bolts or flat-topped head on the monstrous.

## If the results drift

- **Too detailed or realistic:** move "bold simple shapes" to the front, and add "children's party decoration".
- **Texture creeping back in:** add "vector illustration" before "flat lithographed colour".
- **A third or fourth colour appearing:** add "two-colour print" and name the unwanted colour in `--no`.
- **Too cute for the vampires and the monstrous:** swap "big friendly eyes" for "sly eyes".
