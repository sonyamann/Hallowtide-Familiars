# The Spider: draft copy

Draft 2, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya approved. These lines are now in the content files.

Same rules as the moth: "it" throughout, every triggered nature says only what the numbers support, nothing about size, verdicts short.

**Who gets it:** accounts that write threads (replying to themselves) and share links, more than others do.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Spider (with a variant: Of the Ghost Spider, Of the Vampire Spider, Of the Monstrous Spider)
- **Genus:** *Aranea*. Like *Phalaena*, it is one of Linnaeus's original catch-all genera, since broken up. It is feminine, so the binomials are *Aranea spectralis*, *Aranea sanguisuga* and *Aranea consuta*.

## Verdicts

**General**

- `spider.verdict.1` It sayeth nothing in one strand that may be spun into seven.
- `spider.verdict.2` What it catcheth, it keepeth, and bindeth fast to what it caught before.
- `spider.verdict.3` Its web is its argument, and it hath not finished.

**Ghost Spider** (seen but not heard, or given to long silences)

- `spider.verdict.ghost` It spinneth in an empty room, and the web is no worse for that.

**Vampire Spider** (feeds on replies and mentions)

- `spider.verdict.vampire` It maketh every line fast to somebody else, and so is never without company.

**Monstrous Spider** (stitched from links, reposts and quotes)

- `spider.verdict.monstrous` Its web is hung with other creatures' treasures, each bound into place with care.

## Natures

**With a trigger**

| Id | Shown when | Line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `spider.nature.threads` | Replies to its own posts more than most (top quarter) | Having said a thing, it descendeth a little and sayeth the next, and so by degrees reacheth the floor. |
| `spider.nature.threads-most` | The same, top tenth | It hath never once been content with a single strand. |
| `spider.nature.links` | Shares links more than most (top quarter) | It runneth lines to distant places, and whatsoever is far off, it bringeth near. |
| `spider.nature.links-most` | The same, top tenth | No corner of the world is so remote that it hath not made a line fast to it. |
| `spider.nature.length` | Its posts run long on average (top quarter) | It measureth its sayings by the yard, and not the inch. |

**Fallbacks, with no trigger**

- `spider.nature.general.1` It is patient beyond the custom of the age, and will finish what it began though none remain to watch.
- `spider.nature.general.2` Its work is swept away by morning, and it beginneth again without complaint.
- `spider.nature.general.3` It denieth having said anything about the Moth.

## Notes

- **The third fallback answers the moth's joke.** It only makes sense beside it, so it works as a reward for people comparing cards, and is merely odd otherwise. Cut it if that bothers you.
