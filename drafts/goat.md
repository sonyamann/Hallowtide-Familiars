# The Goat: draft copy

Draft 1, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya edited. These lines are now in the content files.

**Who gets it:** accounts whose posts get quoted more, relative to likes, than other people's. The scapegoat: what it says gets carried off and argued over.

**The risk in the lore:** "draws the crowd's ire" could read as "people dislike you". The measure can't tell ire from praise, so every line says only that its words get carried off and disputed, and the goat is never bothered by it.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Goat (Of the Ghost Goat, Of the Vampire Goat, Of the Monstrous Goat)
- **Genus:** *Capra*, the Latin for goat and the classic genus. It is feminine: *Capra spectralis*, *Capra sanguisuga*, *Capra consuta*.

## Verdicts

**General**

- `goat.verdict.1` Whatsoever it sayeth, the village carrieth off to argue over elsewhere.
- `goat.verdict.2` It is sent into the wilderness weekly, and weekly returneth, chewing.
- `goat.verdict.3` The crowd layeth its quarrels upon this beast. The beast is unperturbed.

**Ghost Goat** (seen but not heard, or given to long silences)

- `goat.verdict.ghost` It was driven out long ago, and is seen yet upon the hill at dusk. It sheweth no distress.

**Vampire Goat** (feeds on replies and mentions)

- `goat.verdict.vampire` It goeth not into the wilderness alone, but butteth into all company.

**Monstrous Goat** (stitched from links, reposts and quotes)

- `goat.verdict.monstrous` It will eat anything, and hath. What it hath eaten may be read in its coat.

| `goat.nature.answered` | Its posts draw replies more than likes (top quarter) | Few pass it by without a word. |
| `goat.nature.head-on` | Replies to others a great deal (top quarter) | It meeteth every comer head-on. |
| `goat.nature.every-season` | Posts often (fastest quarter) | It is heard upon the hill in every season, and will not be hushed. |

**Fallbacks, with no trigger**

- `goat.nature.general.1` It hath horns, and a beard, and opinions, and is ashamed of none of them.
- `goat.nature.general.2` The sins laid upon it are chiefly other people's. It hath a few of its own, of which it is fond.
- `goat.nature.general.3` It hath eaten the Spider's web twice, and found it wanting salt.

## Notes

- **"Sins"** in the second fallback is the scapegoat ritual, played for a joke. Cut it if it reads as an accusation.
