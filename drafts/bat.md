# The Bat: draft copy

Draft 1, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya edited. These lines are now in the content files.

**Who gets it:** accounts that ask many questions and whose posts draw replies more than likes. It finds its way by echoes.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Bat (Of the Ghost Bat, Of the Vampire Bat, Of the Monstrous Bat)
- **Genus:** *Vespertilio*, "the evening creature", Linnaeus's original catch-all genus for every bat. It is masculine: *Vespertilio spectralis*, *Vespertilio sanguisuga*, *Vespertilio consutus*.

## Verdicts

**General**

- `bat.verdict.1` It crieth out into the dark, and steereth by what cometh back.
- `bat.verdict.2` It asketh, and the night answereth. By this alone it shall find its way.
- `bat.verdict.3` It knoweth the shape of every room by the questions it hath thrown at the walls.

**Ghost Bat** (seen but not heard, or given to long silences)

- `bat.verdict.ghost` It crieth into the dark and the echo is slow in coming; yet it flieth on, and striketh nothing.

**Vampire Bat** (feeds on replies and mentions)

- `bat.verdict.vampire` Of this one the common report is, for once, entirely true.

**Monstrous Bat** (stitched from links, reposts and quotes)

- `bat.verdict.monstrous` It is part mouse, part bird, and part borrowed umbrella. Yet the creature flieth.

| `bat.nature.answered` | Its posts draw replies more than likes (top quarter) | Where others are nodded at, it is answered. |
| `bat.nature.answered-most` | The same, top tenth | It seldom crieth out but that a crowd of voices crieth back. |
| `bat.nature.calls` | Often mentions others by name in its posts (top quarter) | It is forever calling some other creature by name into the dark. |

**Fallbacks, with no trigger**

- `bat.nature.general.1` It hangeth upside down, and from that vantage findeth the world's opinions no stranger than its own.
- `bat.nature.general.2` It is blind only in the proverb. In truth it seeth well enough, and asketh anyway.
- `bat.nature.general.3` The learned dispute whether it be bird or beast. When asked, the Bat answereth with a question.

## Notes

- **The Vampire Bat verdict is a joke about the name,** and says nothing about the account. It is the only verdict like that; I think it earns its place.
- **"Borrowed umbrella"** is an anachronism for a medieval bestiary. It made me laugh, so I left it for you to judge.
