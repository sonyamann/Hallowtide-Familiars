## The four humours

Each humour shows a score from I to V and one short line. The lowest band of each is written as a temperament, never as a failing, so nobody is told their posts do poorly.

**Sanguine** (how warmly its posts are received, for the size of its audience)

| Score | Line |
| --- | --- |
| I | Cool of blood. It seeketh no applause. |
| II | Temperate. Applause findeth it now and then. |
| III | Warm. It is well received. |
| IV | Ruddy. Company doth gather to hear its speech. |
| V | Full-blooded. It cannot speak but the room attendeth. |

**Choleric** (how much it replies to and quotes others)

| Score | Line |
| --- | --- |
| I | No bile to speak of. It letteth others be. |
| II | A little heat, soon spent. |
| III | Quick to answer. |
| IV | Hot. Few sayings may pass unanswered. |
| V | All bile. It answereth everything, and some things twice. |

**Melancholic** (long posts and threads)

| Score | Line |
| --- | --- |
| I | Light of heart, and brief of speech. |
| II | Occasionally given to expounding. |
| III | Pensive. It dwelleth on a matter. |
| IV | Brooding. One saying shall seldom suffice. |
| V | Black bile entire. It writeth at length, and then appendeth. |

**Phlegmatic** (how even its daily posting is)

| Score | Line |
| --- | --- |
| I | Its cries pour forth in floods, then a drought. |
| II | Changeable as the weather. |
| III | Tolerably even. |
| IV | Steady. It keepeth its rhythm. |
| V | Unmoved. It appeareth as doth the tide. |

## Notes

- **"Miserly"** is the spec's own word for the lowest generosity band. It is the one line here that teases the reader. Soften it to "A frugal familiar" if you'd rather it didn't.
- **With today's roster, "Very rare" and "Uncommon" are little used.** Most variant results fall between 2% and 8%, and only the black cat is common.
