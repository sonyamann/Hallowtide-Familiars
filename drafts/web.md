## Errors

- `errors.invalid_handle` That is no handle the scribes can read. Try a name such as @sonyasupposedly.
- `errors.opted_out` This creature hath asked not to be recorded, and the scribes honour it.
- `errors.no_specimen` The scribes found nothing to observe. The account may be private, empty, or not there at all.
- `errors.too_many` The scribes' quills grow weary. Wait an hour, then name another creature.
- `errors.resting` The scribes have observed all the creatures they can for one day. Return on the morrow.
- `errors.failed` The scribes' instruments failed them. Try again in a little while.
- `errors.not_found` No such page is kept in this Bestiary.
