# The Toad: draft copy

Draft 1, Oct 5, 2026. Claude drafted; Sonya approved. These lines are now in the content files.

**Who gets it:** accounts whose best post far outshines their typical one.

**The lore, and the risk in it:** the old belief is that the toad is "ugly and venomous" but carries a jewel in its head. I kept the jewel and wrote the rest as plainness, never ugliness, so no line insults the ordinary posts.

## Name

- **Title:** Of the Toad (Of the Ghost Toad, Of the Vampire Toad, Of the Monstrous Toad)
- **Genus:** *Bufo*, the Latin word for toad and the classic toad genus. It is masculine, so the binomials are *Bufo spectralis*, *Bufo sanguisuga* and *Bufo consutus*.
- **Alternative:** *Rubeta*, the venomous toad of Pliny and the medieval bestiaries. It is more in period and feminine (*Rubeta consuta*), but few will recognise it.

## Verdicts

**General**

- `toad.verdict.1` It sitteth plainly in the mud a twelvemonth, and then is found to have a jewel in its head.
- `toad.verdict.2` Most days it croaketh as any toad; but on a rare day the whole pond falleth silent to hear.
- `toad.verdict.3` Judge it not by its common hours. The jewel is real.

**Ghost Toad** (seen but not heard, or given to long silences)

- `toad.verdict.ghost` It sitteth so still that it is taken for a stone, jewel and all.

**Vampire Toad** (feeds on replies and mentions)

- `toad.verdict.vampire` Its jewel it keepeth at home; its appetite it taketh abroad.

**Monstrous Toad** (stitched from links, reposts and quotes)

- `toad.verdict.monstrous` The jewel is its own. The setting it hath assembled from whatever came to hand.

| `toad.nature.jewel` | Its best post far outdoes its typical one (top quarter) | Among its many common utterances there lieth one that all the country came to see. |
| `toad.nature.jewel-most` | The same, top tenth | The distance between its common day and its best is greater than in almost any creature known. |
| `toad.nature.steady` | Posts at an even daily rhythm (steadiest quarter) | It croaketh at an even measure, wet or dry. |
| `toad.nature.brief` | Its posts are short on average (shortest quarter) | Its sayings are short, as befitteth a creature with no neck. |
| `toad.nature.buried` | Has gone a long while between posts (top quarter) | It burieth itself in the mud for long seasons, and is presumed lost, but is not. |

**Fallbacks, with no trigger**

- `toad.nature.general.1` The jewel in its head is called the toadstone, and is proof against poison. It will not say how it came by it.
- `toad.nature.general.2` It hath been called ugly by poets, who are not themselves much to look at.
- `toad.nature.general.3` It regardeth the Moth's nightly errands with pity, and the Spider's industry with alarm.
